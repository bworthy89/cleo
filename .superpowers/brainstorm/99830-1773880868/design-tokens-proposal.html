Vibe System

The existing 11 vibes remain. Each vibe overrides: background, text, and secondary accent. The primary accent (#C8832A gold) stays constant across all vibes — it's Cleo's identity. The vibe accent colors shift the secondary elements (progress bars, active states, mood indicators).
