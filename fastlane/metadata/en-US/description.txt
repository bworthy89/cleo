ONAY is your personal AI radio host. Pick a playlist, choose a vibe, and ONAY transforms it into a live radio broadcast — with voice commentary between every track.

No more silent playlist shuffling. ONAY listens to what's playing and delivers real radio moments: artist callouts, track intros, genre bridges, and mid-song drops that make every session feel alive.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick any Apple Music playlist or station
• Choose a vibe — chill, workout, lateNight, party, and more
• ONAY takes over as your AI host, speaking between tracks with context-aware commentary
• Smooth radio-style crossfades blend ONAY's voice with the music

FEATURES
• 12 broadcast vibes that shape ONAY's tone and energy
• Smart transitions — ONAY knows the artist, the genre, and what just played
• Session memory — ONAY remembers your listening history across sessions
• Editorial insight cards with producer credits and song context
• Session arc visualization so you can see your broadcast unfold
• Broadcast archives to revisit past sessions

DESIGNED FOR LISTENERS
ONAY isn't a chatbot. It's a radio host. Every word is crafted for the ear — natural delivery, warm timing, and zero filler. The experience is built around the music, not the AI.
