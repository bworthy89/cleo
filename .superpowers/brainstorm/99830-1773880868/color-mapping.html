How should the Stitch colors map to the PRD palette?

A

Single-Accent Gold (Monochromatic)

Replace both Stitch primary (orange) and secondary (cyan) with PRD gold #C8832A. All accents, glows, gradients, and interactive elements use gold at varying opacities. The app becomes a refined black-and-gold experience — like a luxury audio device.

B

Gold Primary + Warm Secondary

Primary accent → PRD gold #C8832A. For the secondary (where Stitch uses cyan), derive a warm complementary — like a deep amber #D4A55A or warm cream #E8D5B5. Keeps the two-tone depth of the Stitch designs without the cold cyan.

C

Gold Primary + Keep Vibe System

Default state uses gold #C8832A as the primary accent. But preserve your existing 11-vibe system from design-tokens — the secondary accent color dynamically shifts based on the active vibe (purple for Late Night, red for Workout, etc). Best of both worlds: PRD identity + dynamic personality.
