A

Keep PRD Typography

Playfair Display (headers), Work Sans (body), EB Garamond (Cleo voice), DM Mono (labels). Preserve the editorial, magazine-like feel. The serif headlines are distinctive and different from typical music apps.

B

Adopt Stitch Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans (headlines), Inter (body/labels). Modern, tech-forward, tight tracking. Matches the glassmorphism aesthetic better. Loses the editorial personality but gains a more premium audio-tech feel.

C

Hybrid — Best of Both

Playfair Display for large display text (track titles, screen headers). Inter for body/UI text (cleaner than Work Sans for small sizes). EB Garamond stays for Cleo's voice (it's her signature). DM Mono stays for technical labels. Stitch layout + PRD personality.
