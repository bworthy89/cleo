How Cleo Speaking Works

The speaking state is an overlay on top of the Broadcast screen, not a separate screen. It activates when Cleo has something to say between tracks.

Activation Sequence
1
Track ends — onTrackChanged fires, 1.5s natural pause
2
Broadcast screen dims — blur(4px), grayscale(30%), opacity 30%. Controls gray out.
3
Disruption overlay slides in — "HOST INTERJECTION" title with glitch effect, then quote box rotated -1.5deg
4
Words highlight sequentially — each word in the quote bolds/highlights as Cleo speaks it via TTS (synced to audio)
5
TTS finishes — overlay dissolves (600ms), broadcast un-dims, music resumes at full volume
6
Commentary card persists — the glass commentary card on the normal broadcast screen now shows the quote that was just spoken, so you can read it back
Replaces

PullQuoteOverlay + WordByWordSubtitle

The current PullQuoteOverlay (full black backdrop + clause animation) and WordByWordSubtitle (staggered word fade) merge into this single disruptive state. More dramatic, more Cleo.

Design Details

Visual Treatment

JetBrains Mono 800 for the "HOST INTERJECTION" title (glitch text-shadow). Quote box has the tilted -1.5deg rotation from Stitch. Scanline animation sweeps across. Signal metadata in the corner adds broadcast authenticity.

Segment Types

When It Triggers

This full disruptive state triggers for track_story and post_track_reflection segments. Lighter segments (transitions, fun facts) use the commentary card on the normal broadcast screen without the full takeover — just the waveform badge + EB Garamond text.
